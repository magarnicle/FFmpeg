Catch-up / nDVR caption issue ("start from beginning") - findings
=================================================================

SYMPTOM
On the set-top box "start from beginning" (always-recording catch-up) feature:
- Live broadcast teletext decodes fine on the STB (both Polistream and DeckLink).
- Catch-up captions are NOT styled like teletext, so the headend converts the VBI
  teletext into a rendered subtitle format (DVB bitmap subtitles, DVB teletext, or a
  text sidecar) when it records. That transcoder is the point of failure.
- With Polistream as the source, catch-up captions appear and update correctly.
- With our DeckLink source, the caption box flashes at the correct times but the text
  is frozen on the last caption Polistream output before the switch. Switching back to
  Polistream, captions update again immediately.
- Not known whether catch-up has EVER worked with the DeckLink source.

MECHANISM
The transcoder is receiving our caption timing/page events (box flashes) but is not
applying our text rows to its page memory, so it keeps rendering the last complete page
it decoded (Polistream's). Control/header bytes are Hamming-coded (error-correctable, so
the box/timing survives); text is parity-only (a bit error discards the character), but
that is not the cause here (see below).

RULED OUT (measured from SDI captures deck*.txt vs poli*.txt, decoded with
teletext_tools/vbi_compare.py; our output matches Polistream on all of these):
- Waveform / decode reliability: our text rows decode at 0% parity errors even off
  optimal timing; vertical eye is on par with Polistream.
- Packet format: page 801, magazine 8, framing 0x27, double-height, Start/End boxing -
  identical.
- Header control bits: byte7 0xD0 (C6=1), byte8 0x2F (C7/C8/C9=1), C11=0, region 0 -
  identical.
- Subcode: both hold P801 subcode at 0 (no per-caption change).
- Dual-field split: both distribute header + row20 + row22 across the two fields the
  same way (row20 on one field, row22 on the other).
- Header/erase frequency: our old ratio was header:row 1.47:1 with many erase headers vs
  Polistream 0.84:1 with few; -teletext_defer_erase brings this toward Polistream, but it
  did NOT fix catch-up.

CONFIG CHANGES TRIED
- -teletext_header_once 1: BROKE live teletext (header dropped to ~8 in 500 frames; a
  teletext decoder needs the header every carousel cycle). Do NOT use. Reverted.
- -teletext_defer_erase 1: live teletext OK, but catch-up still frozen. Did not fix it.

CONCLUSION
The difference that breaks the transcoder is not visible in the SDI packet captures - our
teletext matches Polistream's in every measurable respect and decodes cleanly. The cause
is therefore either in the real-time transmission cadence (which the frame-dropping AJA
grab cannot show) or in a transcoder-specific interaction only visible at the headend.

NEXT STEPS (need the service provider / headend)
1. Capture of the transcoded caption stream across a Polistream->DeckLink switch (the
   recorded DVB subtitle / teletext / sidecar). Shows exactly what the transcoder emits
   for each source and where it stops updating text. This is the definitive diagnostic.
2. Transcoder make/model and the catch-up caption format. Its spec states what it expects
   from the input teletext, to check our stream against directly.

STILL WORTH DOING ON OUR SIDE
- Plain-config test: -teletext_continuous 1 -teletext_filler dummy -teletext_dual_field 0
  -teletext_shape 0 -teletext_vbi_offset 6 -double_height 1. If catch-up works with any
  config, a specific feature is the cause; re-enable one at a time.
- ffmpeg -loglevel debug log (the "Teletext:" lines) to characterise our exact per-frame
  cadence, which the grab hides, in case of a transmission-timing anomaly.
